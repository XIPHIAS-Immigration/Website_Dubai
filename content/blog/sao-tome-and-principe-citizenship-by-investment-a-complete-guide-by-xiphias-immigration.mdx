### **São Tomé CBI Requirements**

To apply for citizenship in São Tomé and Príncipe through investment, you need to meet a few key requirements:

**[The Key requirements of São Tomé CBI ](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.ae/residency)**

- **Minimum investment:** US$90,000 for a single applicant (US$95,000 for a family of two to four, plus US$5,000 for each additional dependant) as a non-refundable contribution, plus a US$5,000 non-refundable submission fee

- **Family inclusion:** Citizenship can extend to your spouse, dependent children and dependent parents over 55; passport issuance for adult dependent children aged 18 and over is currently on hold pending a revised dependency law

- **No need to be there:** The whole process can be done remotely without visiting the country

- **Clean record:** All applicants must provide verified documents and pass background checks

- **Nationality limit:** The nationality law bars applicants who already hold more than two other nationalities

### **Understanding the São Tomé Citizenship Process**

Getting a São Tomé passport is simple and clear. The steps are:

1. **Submit your application:** Apply through the Citizenship Investment Unit (CIU) and pay the submission fee.

2. **Preliminary approval:** The CIU reviews your application and approves it in principle.

3. **Pay the contribution:** Complete the non-refundable contribution.

4. **Receive your passport:** Once citizenship is granted, your passport is issued. The unit's first-year report records an average of about two months to approval and about four months to passport.

The programme has a single investment route:
