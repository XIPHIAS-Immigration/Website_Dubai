**Alberta Express Entry Stream**

The Alberta Express Entry Stream is part of the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP), which was called the Alberta Immigrant Nominee Program (AINP) until 2022. It's a passive program indicating that you apply to this program only when invited. Unless you get a notification from the AAIP, you can't apply to this program.

If selected, you receive an invitation through your Express Entry portal. Invited applicants are given a specific number of days to apply for a provincial nomination to the AAIP. In case you don't meet the deadline, your invitation gets canceled, according to CIC News.

#### You may receive an invitation (Notification of Interest) if:

- Your profile is active with the Federal Express Entry Pool.

- You have expressed interest in permanently settling down in Alberta.

- You have work expertise in an occupation that is of economic importance in Alberta.

- Your Comprehensive Ranking System score is a minimum of 300.

#### Your chances of getting invited increase if:

- You have work expertise in Alberta or an offer of employment from Alberta.

- You have completed your studies at an academic institute in Canada.

- You have a blood relative, parent, child, or sibling living in Alberta.

#### You may not receive an invitation if:

- The expiry of your Express Entry profile is in 3 months or less

- You work in any ineligible occupation.

#### In 2019, candidates in the following occupations also might not receive an invitation:

NOC 7293- Insulators and NOC 7271-Scaffolders for the duration of work which is more than 6 months.

Occupations that receive a high volume of applications through other AINP streams like:

- 6311- Food Service Supervisors

- 6211- Retail sales supervisors

- 6322- Cooks

- 0621- Retail and wholesale trade managers

- 1241- Administrative assistants

- 1221- Administrative officers

- 1311- Accounting technicians & bookkeepers

- 0631- Restaurant & Food Service managers

- 4214- Early childhood educators & assistants

- 4212- Social & Community service workers

Alberta updates these lists regularly and now uses the 2021 National Occupational Classification (NOC), so check the current list of ineligible occupations on alberta.ca before you apply.
