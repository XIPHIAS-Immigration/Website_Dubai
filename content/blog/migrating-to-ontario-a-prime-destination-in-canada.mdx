Canada runs a wide range of federal and provincial immigration programs and continues to admit hundreds of thousands of new permanent residents every year.

Most of the immigration programs provide Permanent residency in Canada. One of the **best ways to get PR in Canada** is through a provincial nomination. Canada has many provinces which invite immigrants directly into the province through their own immigration program. These provinces have the right to choose the right personnel in their province who can choose the best for the province’s requirements. One such province is Ontario.

Ontario is one of the richest provinces in Canada. It is the house of Canada's capital Ottawa and the famous Toronto city. Toronto is known to be the most technologically advanced city in Canada and it is the technical hub of Canada. This province is best known for all kinds of technical and innovative ideas in Canada. Ontario invites immigrants into the country through their **Provincial Nominee Program Canada**. Ontario is also famous for a number of large water bodies in the province including Niagara Falls. Ontario has many manufacturing industries in the province and also has a large tract of farmland in the province.

The **Ontario immigration program** is formally called the Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP). There are numerous pathways that Ontario has introduced for different kinds of immigrants. Toronto is the main economic hub of Canada. Ontario’s Ottawa is the political center of Canada. Job opportunities in the province are varied, especially in its major cities.

The residents in Ontario are one of the top-earning individuals in comparison to other provinces. Although the cost of living in a city like Toronto is high, it is lower in other parts of the province. Ontario also offers good living conditions. The place is known for cleanliness, safety, and availability of bare necessity services to all the citizens and residents of Ontario. It is one of the reasons why Ontario receives more new immigrants than any other province.

Ontario for one of its immigration pathways works in tandem with **Canada's immigration express entry** system. Canada’s express entry system is a system that was introduced by Canada in the year 2015 to ease the immigration process helping both immigration authorities and the aspirants. The Express Entry system filters the profiles based on their score. Under the Comprehensive Ranking System, profiles are scored on factors such as age, education, work experience and language ability. To enter the pool under the Federal Skilled Worker Program, candidates must first score at least 67 points on the federal selection grid. Based on the score, the profiles will be ranked. All those profiles with the highest ranking possible will be considered for further processing.
