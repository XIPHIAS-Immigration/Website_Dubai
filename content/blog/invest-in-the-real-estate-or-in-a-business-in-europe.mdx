## **French Talent Visa Program**

If you are a businessman looking to expand your web into the markets of Europe, one of the best modes could be the **French Talent Visa program**. The Talent Passport is a multi-year residence permit of up to four years, which can be renewed. You will need to provide accurate documents, including your experience as a businessman, your purpose for the visa and your business plan for the organization in France. The business creation route requires at least EUR 30,000 of project financing, and the economic investment route a direct investment of at least EUR 300,000. After five years of legal residence, you can apply for a 10-year resident card, and five years of residence is also the general requirement for French naturalisation. This is one of the top investment opportunities as France itself is a country popular for fashion apart from it providing the required infrastructure for conducting business there.

## **Portugal Golden Visa Program**

The **Portugal Golden Visa Program** is one of the top attractors for investors. This visa allows you access to one of the historical countries in Europe. Portugal has defined multiple investment programs in the country to fund their numerous projects. Portugal ended its real estate route in October 2023. The main investment route today is a subscription of at least EUR 500,000 in a qualifying investment fund; other routes include company capitalisation and research or cultural contributions. The program does not require the investor to live in Portugal for long: the minimum stay is 7 days in the first year and 14 days in each later two-year period. After five years you can apply for permanent residence; Portuguese citizenship now requires 10 years of residence for Indian nationals.

## **Malta Residency Program**

**Malta PR program** is designed for those who buy or rent qualifying real estate and make a contribution to Malta. This program allows the investor to settle and live in Malta indefinitely. To qualify, you buy a property worth at least EUR 375,000 or rent one for at least EUR 14,000 a year, and keep it for at least five years. You also pay an administrative fee of EUR 60,000 and a government contribution of EUR 37,000, make a EUR 2,000 donation to a registered NGO, and show capital of at least EUR 500,000 (of which EUR 150,000 in financial assets) or EUR 650,000 (of which EUR 75,000 in financial assets). Malta’s citizenship by investment scheme was repealed in 2025 and is no longer available.

## **Cyprus Residency Program**

Cyprus offers permanent residence to investors who buy qualifying property and meet an income requirement. XIPHIAS confirms the current investment threshold and conditions with the Civil Registry and Migration Department before you commit funds. Cyprus is in the EU but not in the Schengen Area, so a Cypriot residence permit does not give Schengen travel rights, and Cyprus ended its citizenship by investment program on 1 November 2020.

## **Ireland VISA program**

Ireland invites entrepreneurs to start a business in Ireland through its **Start-up Entrepreneur Programme (STEP)**. Applicants need at least EUR 50,000 in funding for an innovative, high-potential start-up that can create 10 jobs in Ireland and reach EUR 1 million in sales within three to four years. Permission is first granted for two years and can then be renewed for three more years, and time in Ireland can count towards Irish citizenship, which requires five years of reckonable residence. Corporate tax in Ireland is among the lowest in Europe.
