Govt of Canada Job Bank portal also has a verification process for an employer to register and post jobs. Data is validated from Canada Revenue Agency records. This ensures only genuine jobs and employers exist in the system, unlike other private portals.

Any eligible job offer made to non-Canadians also qualifies them to get additional points in the Express Entry Immigration Pool system for faster possibilities of Permanent migration to Canada with the whole family. Yes, it means in many situations people have a job secured in hand even before moving to Canada. No need to waste time and money with employment agencies and the least chance of any fraud through the Govt of Canada Job Bank. Since 25 March 2025, a job offer no longer adds Comprehensive Ranking System points in Express Entry, although it can still matter for some programs.
