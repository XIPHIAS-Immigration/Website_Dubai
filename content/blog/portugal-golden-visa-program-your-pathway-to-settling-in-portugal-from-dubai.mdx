# Portugal Golden Visa from Dubai

Have you ever dreamed of living in a picturesque European country? With the Portugal Golden Visa program, your dreams can become a reality. This popular initiative offers a unique opportunity for individuals and families from Dubai and other parts of the world to obtain residency in Portugal and enjoy all the benefits that come with it.

The **Portugal Golden Visa** program, designed to attract foreign investment, allows applicants to invest in the country through various pathways. It provides a host of benefits in return including:

- Visa-free travel within the Schengen Zone

- Access to high-quality healthcare and education systems

- Path to Portuguese citizenship. Whether you're looking for a holiday home, a retirement destination, or a sound investment, the Golden Visa program has something to offer.

Portugal, known for its rich history, stunning landscapes, and vibrant culture, has become an increasingly popular destination for expatriates. And with the Golden Visa program, it's easier than ever to make Portugal your second home. So, if you're ready to turn your dreams into reality, explore the possibilities the Golden Visa program has to offer.

## Benefits of the Portugal Golden Visa Program

The Golden Visa program in Portugal offers a wide range of benefits that make it an attractive option for individuals and families. One of the most significant advantages is the ability to travel freely within the Schengen Zone. This means that once you obtain residency through the Golden Visa program, you can visit other European countries without the need for additional visas or permits. Whether you want to explore the historic streets of Rome, relax on the beaches of Greece, or indulge in the culinary delights of France, the Golden Visa program provides you with the freedom to do so.

In addition to visa-free travel, the Golden Visa program also grants participants access to Portugal's high-quality healthcare and education systems. Portugal boasts a robust healthcare system that is ranked among the best in the world, ensuring that you and your family will receive top-notch medical care. The country also offers a wide range of educational opportunities, from prestigious universities to international schools, making it an ideal destination for families with children.

Furthermore, the Golden Visa program provides participants with the potential for a path to Portuguese citizenship. After meeting the necessary requirements and holding residency for a certain period of time, individuals can apply for citizenship, granting them all the rights and privileges of a Portuguese citizen. This opens up a world of possibilities, from voting in local elections to enjoying the benefits of being a European Union citizen.

## Portugal Golden Visa Requirements and Investment Options

Buying real estate no longer qualifies: Portugal removed the property routes from its Golden Visa in October 2023. Applicants have the flexibility to select one option from any of the following categories:

1. **Capital Transfer:**

- Invest €500,000 in units of an investment fund or venture capital fund dedicated to the growth of Portuguese companies registered under national law. These funds should have a minimum maturity of five years and at least 60% of their portfolio invested in companies with a presence in Portugal (excluding real estate entities).

- Allocate €500,000 for research activities conducted by public or private entities within Portugal's scientific and technological system.

- Contribute €250,000 to support artistic production or the preservation of Portugal's cultural heritage (€200,000 in regions with low population density)

2. **Business Investment:**

- Create a minimum of ten new jobs

- Invest €500,000 in establishing a commercial company registered and headquartered in Portugal, while generating at least five permanent jobs over a three-year period. Alternatively, increase the share capital of an existing Portuguese company, resulting in the creation of at least five permanent jobs or the retention of at least ten jobs for three years.

Please note that these options are part of the Portugal Golden Visa program, designed to promote investment and economic growth in the country.

*A low population density area is defined as less than 100 inhabitants per km² or with a GDP per capita below 75% of the national average.

1. Research and consult with professionals: Before you begin the application process, it is important to do thorough research and seek advice from professionals who specialize in Golden Visa applications. They can provide valuable insights and guide you through the entire process.

2. Choose an investment option: Determine which investment option suits your needs and budget. Whether you decide to invest in a fund, set up a company, or support research or the arts, make sure you understand the requirements and implications of each option.

3. Gather the necessary documents: Prepare all the required documents, such as proof of investment, criminal record clearance, financial statements, and health insurance coverage. It is crucial to have these documents in order to ensure a smooth application process.

4. Submit the application: Once you have gathered all the necessary documents, submit your application to the Portuguese authorities. It is important to follow the guidelines provided and provide accurate and complete information.

5. Await approval: After submitting your application, the authorities will review it and make a decision. The processing time can vary, but it is important to be patient and await the outcome.

6. Obtain residency and fulfill requirements: If your application is approved, you will receive a residency permit. It is important to fulfill the requirements set by the Golden Visa program, such as maintaining your investment for the specified period of time and renewing your residency permit when necessary.

7. Apply for citizenship (optional): After holding residency for a certain period of time, you may be eligible to apply for Portuguese citizenship. This step is optional but provides additional benefits and opportunities.
