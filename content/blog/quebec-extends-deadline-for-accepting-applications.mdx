In order to give candidates the opportunity to create their account and to fulfill their demand for a Quebec Selection Certificate for the next period for receipt of applications, this period has been adapted to allow more time for applicants.

So:

- Candidates have until 25 January 2016 to create their account in the secure Mon projet Québec space; after that date, it will not be possible to create a new account for the current reception period;

- between January 26 and February 15, 2016, candidates who have a Mon projet Québec account can access it to complete the online Quebec Selection Certificate Application Form;

- as of February 16, 2016, it will be possible to submit an application and pay the required fees. The receipt of applications period will end March 31, 2016.

The Ministry during this period will receive a maximum of 2,800 applications.
