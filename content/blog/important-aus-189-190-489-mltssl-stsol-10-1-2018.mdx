The Department of Home Affairs publishes the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL) and the Short-term Skilled Occupation List (STSOL), which set the occupations eligible for Australia’s points-tested skilled visas, including subclasses 189 and 190. The lists were updated in January 2018 and change regularly, so check the current lists on the [Department of Home Affairs skilled occupation list page](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/working-in-australia/skill-occupation-list) before you apply.

The subclass 489 visa closed to new applications on 16 November 2019 and was replaced by the [Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491)](/skilled/australia/skilled-work-regional-491).
