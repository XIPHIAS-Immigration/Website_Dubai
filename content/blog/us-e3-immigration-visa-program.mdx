US E3 Immigration Visa Program

The US E3 Visa is an employment-based temporary visa that is available only to Australians. Mainly, it helps Australian businessmen and professionals to capitalize on the offers provided by the Free Trade Agreement (AUSFTA) between Australia and the United States. 10,500 E3 visas are exclusively reserved for Australian nationals every year. This requires sponsorship by a US employer and a certified Labor Condition Application (LCA). The E3 visa holders can go for status adjustment and get a US Green Card. You can change your employer to another one. If your job ends, you may have a grace period of up to 60 days (or until your authorized stay ends, if sooner) to find a new sponsoring employer.

#### Eligibility Criteria:

1. Applicants must be Australian citizens.

2. Applicants must have a genuine job offer from a US employer in a specialty occupation, such as:

- IT Jobs such as analysts, programmers, and so on.

- Business Manager

- Healthcare and medical practitioners

- Educational specialists like teachers, consultants, and managers

- Researchers

3. Applicants must carry necessary academic or other qualifying credentials.

4. Should fill a role qualifying as a specialized occupation.

#### Benefits:

- Quick visa process (1-2 months)

- Renewable indefinitely (per two years)

- Unlimited extensions

- The initial valid stay is 2 years

- It’s filing charges are considerably lower than for other work permits

- High chances of visa approval

- Spouse and unmarried children under the age of 21 years can accompany the E3 visa holder

- It allows the E3 visa-holder spouse to work in the US

#### Apply Procedure:

- Get Sponsorship from a U.S. Employer.

- Complete Form DS-160, Non-immigrant Visa Application

- Make an online fee payment.

- Fix your visa interview

- Keep required documents ready

- Attend the interview
