Each province sets its own criteria to meet its labour market needs. This program is known as the provincial nomination program. Below are a few of the most **popular PNPs in Canada**. The PNP programs were started in the 90s. Canada continues to admit hundreds of thousands of new permanent residents every year, and its 2026 levels plan allocates 91,500 places to the Provincial Nominee Program.

**Alberta PNP** –

The **Alberta Provincial Nominee Program** is now called the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP); it was known as the AINP until 2022. Alberta is one of Canada’s wealthiest provinces and invites skilled professionals to help local employers find the right people. Alberta also has a Self-Employed Farmer Stream for experienced farm managers who invest at least CAD 500,000 in a primary production farm, although very few entrepreneur nominations are available in 2026. Alberta is one of the powerhouses in Canada and thrives on its energy industry.

**Saskatchewan PNP** –

Saskatchewan runs immigration categories for skilled workers, health care professionals and students; its Entrepreneur and Farm Owner categories closed permanently on 27 March 2025. The **Saskatchewan PNP** program allows immigrants to live and work in any part of Saskatchewan. The province is famous for its increasing opportunities, the cost of living, and the lifestyle one can experience there. Saskatchewan provides high-quality education and good medical care for its residents.

**Ontario PNP** –

Ontario is the second-largest province in Canada. Ontario is the financial and technical hub of Canada. It houses Toronto and Ottawa, two of the strongest cities in Canada. Ontario has many industries which include tourism, finance, technology, and manufacturing industries among others. **Ontario’s Provincial Nominee program** is one of the best in the country. The program works in tandem with the Federal Government’s Express Entry System. Ontario’s streams cater to skilled professionals and students; its Entrepreneur Stream for business investors has closed. The living standards in Toronto are top-class.

**British Columbia** –

Another most sought-after province in Canada is British Columbia. It is the westernmost province in Canada. It is surrounded by the Pacific coastline and mountain ranges. The province is famous for its adventurous trails and treks. People come here to explore the beauty and vastness of British Columbia. **British Columbia’s Provincial Nominee Program's** main intention was to attract the right high skilled professionals into the province to ensure economic growth here. BC has defined enough programs to cater to the needs of skilled professionals from highly skilled to medium-range skilled professionals. BC also invites entrepreneurs to set up their business ideas in BC or invest in an already-running business in Canada. BC economy is defined by industries which include tourism, energy, manufacturing industry, film & television industry, construction, and agriculture among others.

**Manitoba** –

Manitoba is another strong province of Canada where original Canadians had settled in large numbers. The province has many rivers and is considered a rich economy due to the prevalence of huge mineral reserves, large tracts of farmland, and huge manufacturing units. Manitoba is made of clean and safe cities. Manitoba offers good living conditions with a relatively low cost of living. The **Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program (MPNP)** has several immigration streams that help immigrants relocate to the province. Skilled professionals, Business Entrepreneurs, investors, and Students can utilize these immigration programs to relocate to the province.

**Atlantic Provinces** –

New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland & Labrador, and Nova Scotia together form the Atlantic Provinces. These provinces lie on Canada’s Atlantic coast. These provinces are beautiful and are surrounded by ocean. Each of the provinces is a specialist in at least one of the ship businesses. They are famous for fishing, farming, shipbuilding, forestry and mining. The cost of living in these provinces is pretty low but one can get to live European kind of living here. These provinces invite businessmen and skilled professionals to relocate here and work as would be possible.
