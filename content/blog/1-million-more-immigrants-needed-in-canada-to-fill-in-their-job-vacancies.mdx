Canada has been a world leader in handling the immigration profiles they have been receiving to fill in their job vacancies. In 2018 alone, Canada admitted more than 300,000 new permanent residents, many of them skilled workers selected through its Provincial Nominee Program and Express Entry system. This systematic work inflow and its professional handle has not only helped the Canadian Federal Government to address the Skilled Human Resource shortage in the country but has also ensured less anxiety and quicker time process of PR for the applicants.

India and China are the largest sources of new permanent residents in Canada. To keep filling labour shortages, Canada's 2019–2021 Immigration Levels Plan set targets of 330,800 new permanent residents in 2019, 341,000 in 2020 and 350,000 in 2021, more than 1 million newcomers over three years.

Most immigration Programs in Canada ensure the immigrants obtain their Canada Permanent Residency status which gives immigrants most of the same rights and benefits as Canadian citizens, including public health care. The living conditions, health benefits, and education in Canada are world-class supported by a stable economy and strong governance.
