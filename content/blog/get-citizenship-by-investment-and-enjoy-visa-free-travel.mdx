### What is Citizenship?  

The status of a person being identified as a legal member of a sovereign state under the custom or law is characterized to be as Citizenship. When a person becomes a citizen of a particular country, she/he will enjoy the benefits of that particular country defined by the law of the country for a citizen.

### What is Citizenship by Investment?

Citizenship by investment programs, such as those run by the [Caribbean countries](/caribbean-citizenship-from-uae) and Turkey, enable investors looking for a second passport to make a qualifying investment, such as a contribution to a government fund or approved real estate, and obtain citizenship in that country.

### Benefits of Citizenship by Investment: –

• Lifetime citizenship, subject to each country’s rules.

• Pass your citizenship for your future generation.

• You can enjoy all the benefits defined by the law of the country i.e. right to vote, free education & health care, opportunity to be part of politics.

• There are no education, language or work-experience requirements, although every applicant must pass strict due diligence checks.

• You get the country’s passport, which gives visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to many countries; the exact list depends on the passport.

Indian nationals should note that India does not allow dual citizenship, so acquiring another citizenship ends Indian citizenship.

### What Does Visa-Free Travel Mean?

Visa-free travel allows you to travel abroad without lengthy visa formalities. If you obtain [citizenship by investment](/citizenship), you can enjoy this benefit in every country that allows visa-free entry to holders of your new passport.

#### Advantages of Visa-Free Travel: –

• You can skip the system of filling out the lengthy form.

• Some destinations only ask for a short online travel authorisation, such as the UK’s ETA.

• You can save the processing time taken for your visa application at the government office or relevant body.

• You can avoid unexpected circumstances that may arise during your travel abroad.

• You can improve your business prospects across the globe
