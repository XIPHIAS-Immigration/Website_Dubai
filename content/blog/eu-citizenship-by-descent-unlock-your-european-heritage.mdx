### **What is EU Citizenship by Descent?**

**EU citizenship by descent** is a way to obtain a European passport through your family lineage rather than residence, employment, or investment. Countries in Europe often allow citizenship claims if you can prove a direct ancestral connection.

### This pathway is particularly appealing because it provides:

- Visa-free travel across the EU and Schengen area

- The ability to work and study anywhere in Europe

- Access to social benefits and healthcare

By leveraging your heritage, you can gain rights equivalent to any citizen born in the country.

### **Which Countries Offer Citizenship Through Ancestry?**

Several European nations provide citizenship through ancestry, though eligibility rules vary. Some popular countries include:

- **Ireland:** Citizenship via parent or grandparent

- **Italy:** Claims through distant generations were sharply restricted in 2025, so most new applicants now need an Italian parent or grandparent

- **Poland:** Citizenship based on descent from Polish ancestors

- **Portugal & Spain:** Allows claims from parents or grandparents, depending on circumstances

Understanding each country’s rules is crucial to ensuring your application meets all requirements.

### **Who is Eligible?**

Eligibility depends on your direct ancestry. Most countries require proof that one of your parents or grandparents was a citizen. Some countries may even consider great-grandparents.

### To qualify, applicants typically need:

- Birth certificates linking you to your ancestor

- Marriage certificates if surnames differ

- Proof that your ancestor retained citizenship at the time of your birth

This is why detailed documentation and proper verification are essential for success in **citizenship eligibility by descent**.
